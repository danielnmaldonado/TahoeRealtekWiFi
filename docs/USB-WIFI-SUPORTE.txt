WHAT SUPPORT MEANS IN THIS LIST
There are 13 integrated station backends and 262 USB identities eligible in the source.
A board must match the required VID:PID, configuration, interface, bcdDevice, and class/subclass/protocol, in addition to initialization checks for silicon, ROM, RF, endpoints, and power.
The commercial product name alone does not identify a board revision. A model sold with a different chipset/VID:PID/revision does not become supported just because it carries the same name.
This is a source-level eligibility list, not physical certification of 262 boards. Every CSV record has hardware_validation set to not_performed.
Historical hardware evidence documented on this PC is limited to RTL8192EU 2357:0108 and RTL8814AU 2357:0106. Build53 was observed loaded during the checkup; build55 requires a separate load. This audit did not access the radio or perform traffic tests.
