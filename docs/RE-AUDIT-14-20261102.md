## What was fixed in code

- CU/88E: HT20/MCS path, QoS, SGI and BA; firmware RA on CU and host CCX adaptation on 88E. CU respects peer SMPS. The old logs for these four captures operate in b/g; speed after the change was not measured.
- B/C: HT40/VHT20/40/80 widths on non-DFS 5 GHz channels, with consistent calibration, RA, descriptors, RX, coexistence and BA agreements. In 2.4 GHz and DFS they remain HT20; channel 165 uses 20 MHz. The five old captures show HT20 without VHT.
- Edimax 7392:a611: four physical OUT endpoints with three DMA queues and provider discovery diagnostics. The old archive publishes 0xe00002c7 (Unsupported) during USB discovery and six total endpoints; it lacks the descriptor needed to prove how many are OUT.
- AU 8812/8821: bounded resident MCU reset and correct reading of 80 MHz power bytes/nibbles according to pinned Linux. It was not demonstrated that 2357:0120 had a resident MCU or that this power error explains its failure.
- Common path: IE cache memory bounds/ownership; HT/VHT advertisement and negotiation respect the registered channel map. Tests demonstrate software contracts without reproducing radio panics.

## What remains open or unproven

1. **Zero native RX:** in the 12 working cases, offered/delivered Ethernet packets increase and native counters stay at 0, with valid KPI reading. Build61 adds diagnostic samples; it contains no confirmed causal fix for this discrepancy.
2. **2357:010c / CCMP:** during the window there are replay+2, decrypt+2 and inputErrors+2. These are overlapping counts of the same rejection path; MIC stays at 0. CCMP61 code is identical to 55. The cause of retransmissions/rejections remains unknown; retaining replay protection is mandatory for connection security.
3. **The two hardware failures:** Edimax 7392:a611 and TPLink 2357:0120 have no new confirmation of search, WPA2 association and traffic on build61. On 0120 the interface is UP without RUNNING, channel/SCAN/RX/WPA do not advance; RadioReady was not recorded and cannot be inferred as 0. The package has no enable/scan return; this does not prove that no attempt occurred.
4. **0120's 2.4GHz:** the published mask does not include this band. Without OTP and power/boot/control results, the cause cannot be selected from calibration, profile, initialization or another failure. No factory value was invented to open channels.
5. **Historical panics:**42 files correspond to 2 events, with conflicting header/body versions and a UUID different from the kext loaded in the captures. The memory fix is in source, but there is no demonstration of causality or proof that all crashes were resolved.
6. **Performance:**none of the 14 logs contains a download/upload benchmark. Last rate/feedback and LinkSpeed=0 do not measure sustained capacity. There is no basis to declare speed fixed for all adapters.

## Counters that must not become false failures

Duplicates increased 17 and below-window BA drops 18, summed only within each category. They are protocol filters, not isolated proof of a defect. RF retries and ignored feedback also do not equate to Ethernet losses. Do not sum overlapping categories as distinct events.

## Collector coverage and fix

Of 560 recorded commands, 541 returned 0; five unified queries reached the deadline and 14 spctl queries printed assessments disabled/exit 1. The latter describes Gatekeeper state, not a radio failure. Data lost to the five old timeouts cannot be reconstructed from these ZIPs.

The collector revision preserves raw spctl results and classifies this expected state separately; a timed-out historical query retains partial files and receives one additional recent attempt, with separate files and coverage. Even when fallback succeeds, the historical window remains partial. The revision passed 30 offline cases (21 originals preserved and 9 new), plus independent review and tests. These tests do not recover events missing from the old files.

The collector copies up to 20 crash reports by name, without an age filter or event deduplication. Presence in each ZIP therefore does not mean a new panic or a link to the adapter being tested at that time. This limit is documented; it was not turned into a driver-fix claim.
