## Global Constraints

- Work directly in the source; preserve app46 and ABI unless a defect is confirmed.
- Do not install/reload the kext, alter EFI, restart or change the network.
- Do not presume the cause of the seven input errors or infer PHY rate from WAN speed.
- Agents change separate blocks; root integrates CMake, versions and delivery.

## Review Focus

- A TX stall must not free a packet that IOKit will retry.
- Retry must not duplicate packets, count an error or wake after teardown.
- RX accounting must follow the Apple contract without duplicating inputPackets/bytes.
- Diagnostics must belong to the controller/association and be published under the gate.
- Invalid or absent rates/BA must remain unknown, without changing RF policy.
